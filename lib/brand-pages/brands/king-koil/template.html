FLOWT by King Koil

A new dimension of rest. King Koil’s FLOWT spiral gel is designed to support, adapt, lift and cool, for weightless comfort while you sleep.

Free airflow

Air moves through the spiral gel in all directions, helping heat escape and fresh air circulate.

Progressive spiral design

The spiral design gently flexes with every movement, easing pressure points and adapting to your body as you shift.

Adaptive cooling

Thermal gel absorbs and releases heat evenly to help keep the mattress balanced and cool.
