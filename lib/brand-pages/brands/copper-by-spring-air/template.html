What makes Copper different

Every Copper by Spring Air mattress pairs copper in the cover with copper in the comfort layers.

01

Copper-infused NatuVerex™ cover

Spring Air’s patented cooling cover is knit with copper yarns. According to Spring Air, NatuVerex™ copper fabrics use a minimum of 30% copper yarn content, providing anti-inflammatory, antiviral and anti-bacterial properties for wellness while you sleep.

02

Copper-infused memory foam

Below the cover, copper-infused memory foam and pressure-relieving comfort foams contour to your shape, so you get cushioning where your body presses into the bed most.

03

Zoned hybrid support

A zoned coil support system sits under the foams to distribute weight and reduce motion, so the mattress responds to how you move through the night.

Inside a Copper hybrid

  1. NatuVerex™ copper-infused cover with a minimum of 30% copper yarn content
  2. Copper-infused memory foam for pressure relief
  3. Comfort foams tuned to each model’s feel: firm, medium or plush
  4. Zoned coil support system (528 coils in a twin up to 1,085 in a king)
